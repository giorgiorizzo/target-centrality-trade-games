Implementing Target Eigenvector Centralities through Noncooperative Network Games:
Theory and an Application to the BACI Trade Network
(M. Passacantando, F. Raciti, G. Rizzo)
 
SUPPLEMENTARY TABLES
====================
 
This file reproduces, in plain text, the tables reported in the Appendix of the paper for the four
experiments carried out on the BACI trade network (sector 870323, HS92): Experiment 1 (target equal to
the baseline), Experiment 2 (random target), Experiment 3.1 (top-5 EU exporters, -10%) and Experiment 3.2
(Italy, +10%). For each experiment the tables report the parameters and aggregate results, the percentage
variation of the target centrality of the members of the coalition (Experiments 2, 3.1 and 3.2 only),
the arcs whose flow varies by more than 100% between baseline and equilibrium, the share of the export
reallocated by each member, and a summary of the multipliers. The first section reports the data and
parameters common to the four experiments (sector, baseline centrality, coalition, calibration of theta* and
check on k), which are not repeated in the complete run outputs available in the same repository.
